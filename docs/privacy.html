Privacy Policy

momeo does not collect anything about you. Your voice and your notes stay on your phone.

This policy describes how the voice notes app "momeo" (the "App") handles information about its users.

Effective July 19, 2026
Last updated November 5, 2026

Personal information

The App does not collect any personal information. No account is required, and the App never asks for your name, email address, location or similar details. The App does not use analytics tools or advertising SDKs.

Microphone and audio

The App uses the microphone to turn what you say into text. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device, and audio is never sent off the device.

The App only records while it is open on your screen. It does not record while you are using other apps or while the screen is off.

Storage of notes

Your notes are stored only on your device. They are not sent to any server, and no one, including the developer, can access their contents.

Sharing with third parties

Because the App does not collect any information about you, it does not share any information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact address on the Support page.
